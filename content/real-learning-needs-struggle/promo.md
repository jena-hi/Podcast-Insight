Helping might be the most selfish thing you do all day.

On this week's Unscripted Intelligence, four of us admitted the same thing: we step in to "save" people, and most of the time it is about our own need for control, not their growth. Arvin Ross said it plainly: when he traces his urge to help, only about 20 percent is love. The rest is control.

Here is the unavoidable truth. Real learning needs struggle you can't manufacture. We build the human skills that matter in the exact moments we are tempted to skip, and every time we rescue too soon, we steal that moment.

So before you jump in next time, ask one question: what would I be robbing them of?
