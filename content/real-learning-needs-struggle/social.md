## Dr. Brian Arnold: AI, ego, and the hero problem  (24:51–25:38)
_Hero gratification and what AI threatens to take away_

### Linkedin
"We tell ourselves it is about the student. Sometimes it is about getting to be the hero."

Dr. Brian Arnold named something uncomfortable on Unscripted Intelligence: a lot of our "help" fills our bucket, not theirs.

Here is the AI twist. As AI removes the everyday struggle we used to swoop in and solve, it also threatens the gratification of being the rescuer. So who are we when we no longer get to save the day?

That is a question worth sitting with.

## Megan Freeman: I mortgaged my home, then almost robbed my team  (13:44–14:50)
_Founded a school, then caught herself rescuing her staff: robbed them and robbed herself_

### Linkedin
I mortgaged my home to start a school. Then I almost ran it the same way I parented my teenagers.

Megan Freeman got honest on Unscripted Intelligence. As her school grew and she hired a team, she kept stepping in to save them from the pain she had already lived through. It felt like leadership. It was really control.

It robbed them of the chance to do it their way. And it robbed her of learning there might be a better way.

Where is your "help" quietly capping the people you lead?
