[01:12] GUEST: Thanks for having me. The honest answer is most of the value isn't

[04:30] HOST: Let's talk about prospecting first, because that's where everyone

[05:02] GUEST: The win is research depth. Before a call, a rep can have a

win use it to send fewer, sharper messages.

[12:45] HOST: What about the call itself? Are people using live AI assist?

[13:20] GUEST: Some are, and it's mixed. Real-time transcription and post-call
summaries are a clear win — reps stop scribbling and actually listen. Live
"whisper" suggestions during a call? Most reps find them distracting. The sweet
spot today is after the call: auto-summary, auto-CRM-update, drafted follow-up.

[21:10] HOST: Let's get into the thing everyone's scared to say out loud — does
this shrink the sales team?

[22:05] GUEST: Short term, no. Medium term, the shape changes. You need fewer
people doing admin and more people who are genuinely good at the human part —
discovery, trust, complex deals. The reps who treat AI as a junior assistant
thrive. The ones who think it's a threat and ignore it fall behind. Skills, not
headcount, is the real story.

[31:40] HOST: If a sales leader is listening and they've done nothing yet, what's
the first move?

[32:15] GUEST: Pick one workflow — call summaries and follow-up. Don't boil the
ocean. Get one thing working, measure the time saved, build trust, then expand.
The failures I see are all "we bought a platform and mandated it." The wins are
all "we solved one annoying problem and reps asked for more."
